# HOSPITAL OPERATIONAL POLICY

**Document ID:** MAN-CMP-004  
**Title:** Workplace Violence Prevention and Anti-Harassment Standards  
**Domain:** Compliance Manual  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope

### Clause 1.1: Objective
This Manual (MAN) establishes operational standards, zero-tolerance guidelines, and protective response workflows to prevent, evaluate, and mitigate incidents of workplace violence, physical assault, verbal threats, stalking, and harassment across all health system environments.

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
This policy applies to all healthcare personnel, attending staff, nursing staff, administrative employees, security officers, volunteers, contractors, and visitors operating across all inpatient, outpatient, and administrative facilities.

---

## Section 2: Roles and Responsibilities

### Clause 2.1: Primary Responsible Role
The **Workplace Safety and Security Committee** is responsible for conducting facility risk assessments, reviewing violence incident reports, coordinating environmental safety controls, and updating security protocols.

### Clause 2.2: Departmental Safety Lead
**Department Managers and Nurse Supervisors** are responsible for ensuring immediate clinical support following violent events, enforcing zero-tolerance standards, and reporting all physical or verbal threats to Security and HR.

---

## Section 3: Prohibited Behaviors and Risk Categories

### Clause 3.1: Prohibited Acts
The health system strictly prohibits all forms of workplace violence and harassment, including:
1. Physical assault, striking, pushing, or intentional bodily contact.
2. Verbal threats, intimidation, coercion, or menacing language.
3. Sexual harassment, unwanted physical advances, or creation of a hostile work environment.
4. Possession of unauthorized weapons, firearms, or dangerous instruments on hospital property.

### Clause 3.2: High-Risk Care Environments
Specific clinical zones requiring heightened security readiness and de-escalation protocols include the Emergency Department, Behavioral Health Intake, Intensive Care Waiting Rooms, and Outpatient Crisis Centers.

---

## Section 4: Incident Handling and Crisis Escalation Workflow

### Clause 4.1: Active Violence / Threat Response Sequence
When an employee encounters an active physical threat, aggressive individual, or severe verbal harassment, staff must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Maintain a safe physical distance and initiate non-confrontational verbal de-escalation techniques.
2. Activate the internal emergency alarm button or dial **ext. 5555** to announce **"Code Grey"** (Aggressive/Violent Individual).
3. Switchboard broadcasts Code Grey with location; Security Officers and Nurse Supervisors respond immediately to the scene.
4. Escalate to **"Code Silver"** (Active Shooter/Weapon Hostile) or contact external law enforcement (911) if a lethal weapon is displayed.
5. Provide immediate medical evaluation and emergency treatment for any injured staff members or visitors.
6. Quarantine and secure the scene to preserve physical evidence if a crime has occurred.
7. Complete an online Safety Incident Report and Workplace Violence Event Form within **12 hours** of the incident.

---

## Section 5: Post-Incident Support and Zero-Tolerance Enforcement

### Clause 5.1: Critical Incident Stress Debriefing (CISD)
Employees involved in or witnessing traumatic workplace violence incidents must be offered immediate supportive debriefing through the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) within **24 hours** post-event.

### Clause 5.2: Enforcement and Legal Action Support
The health system fully supports legal prosecution of individuals who perpetrate physical violence against healthcare workers. Non-employee perpetrators will be permanently banned from non-emergency hospital property, while employee offenders will face immediate termination and referral to law enforcement.
