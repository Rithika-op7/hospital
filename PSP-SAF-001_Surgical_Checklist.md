# HOSPITAL OPERATIONAL POLICY

**Document ID:** PSP-SAF-001  
**Title:** Correct Site, Correct Procedure, Correct Patient Surgical Checklist  
**Domain:** Patient Safety  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope

### Clause 1.1: Objective
This Patient Safety Protocol (PSP) mandates the three-phase Universal Surgical Checklist procedure to eliminate wrong-site, wrong-procedure, and wrong-person surgical events across all invasive operating procedures.

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
This protocol applies to all surgical team members, including attending surgeons, anesthesiologists, nurse anesthetists, circulating nurses, and scrub technicians operating in any surgical or ambulatory procedure unit.

---

## Section 2: Roles and Responsibilities

### Clause 2.1: Primary Responsible Role
The **Circulating Nurse** is responsible for initiating, leading, and documenting all three phases of the Universal Surgical Checklist in the operating room.

### Clause 2.2: Surgical Team Sign-Off
The **Lead Surgeon** and **Anesthesia Care Provider** must verbally confirm and actively participate in all checklist pause points before proceeding with patient care steps.

---

## Section 3: Pre-Operative Verification and Site Marking

### Clause 3.1: Patient Identity Verification
Before moving the patient into the operating room, the pre-operative nurse must verify patient identity using two unique identifiers (Full Name and Date of Birth) against the surgical consent form and patient wristband.

### Clause 3.2: Surgical Site Marking
1. The surgical site must be marked by the licensed practitioner performing the procedure prior to transferring the patient to the OR suite.
2. The mark must be made using a permanent surgical marker, placed at or near the incision site, and visible after patient draping.
3. For bilateral procedures (e.g., left vs. right extremity), both sites must be clearly marked or designated according to protocol.

---

## Section 4: Three-Phase Universal Checklist Execution

### Clause 4.1: Phase 1 - Sign In (Before Induction of Anesthesia)
The Circulating Nurse and Anesthesia Provider must verbally confirm:
1. Patient identity, surgical site, and exact procedure consent.
2. Surgical site mark presence.
3. Pulse oximeter attachment and functioning.
4. Patient allergy status and airway risk assessment.

### Clause 4.2: Phase 2 - Time Out (Before Skin Incision)
Immediately prior to making the initial surgical incision, the entire surgical team must pause and execute the following sequential Time Out workflow:
1. The Circulating Nurse states aloud: "We are now performing the surgical Time Out."
2. All team members halt non-essential activities and direct attention to the checklist.
3. Confirm all team members have introduced themselves by name and role.
4. Verbally confirm patient identity, correct procedure, and exact anatomical site.
5. Confirm displaying of relevant diagnostic images (X-rays, MRIs) in the OR.
6. Surgeon reviews anticipated critical steps, procedure duration, and unexpected blood loss risk.
7. Anesthesia team reviews patient-specific concerns or monitoring requirements.
8. Nursing team confirms instrument sterilization status and equipment availability.
9. Confirm prophylactic antibiotic administration within **60 minutes** prior to incision (or document if not indicated).

### Clause 4.3: Phase 3 - Sign Out (Before Patient Leaves Operating Room)
Before the surgeon leaves the room, the Circulating Nurse leads the Sign Out:
1. Verbally confirm the final name of the procedure performed.
2. Confirm correct counting of sponges, needles, and instruments.
3. Confirm specimen labeling (reading specimen container labels aloud with patient name and anatomical source).
4. Review key equipment issues or mal-functions to be addressed.
5. Review post-operative recovery plans and critical concerns for PACU transfer.

---

## Section 5: Stop-Work Order and Discrepancy Escalation

### Clause 5.1: Immediate Stop-Work Mandate
If any team member identifies a discrepancy during Sign In, Time Out, or Sign Out (e.g., mismatched consent, missing site mark), procedure preparation must cease immediately.

### Clause 5.2: Escalation Protocol
The procedure may not resume until the discrepancy is fully resolved, confirmed by the Lead Surgeon, and re-verified by the entire surgical team.
