# HOSPITAL OPERATIONAL POLICY

**Document ID:** PSP-SAF-006  
**Title:** Patient Identification Band Verification Protocol  
**Domain:** Patient Safety  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope

### Clause 1.1: Objective
This Patient Safety Protocol (PSP) establishes mandatory procedures for applying, maintaining, and verifying physical patient identification bands using two unique identifiers to prevent misidentification during diagnostic, therapeutic, and surgical interventions.

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
This policy applies to all clinical and administrative personnel, including registration staff, phlebotomists, registered nurses, radiological technologists, physicians, and patient transport officers across all care settings.

---

## Section 2: Roles and Responsibilities

### Clause 2.1: Primary Responsible Role
The **Admitting / Registration Clerk** is responsible for generating, printing, and applying the primary identification wristband upon initial patient entry.

### Clause 2.2: Verification Lead
The **Administering Clinician** (nurse, technician, or physician) performing any intervention is responsible for executing the dual-identifier check prior to initiating care.

---

## Section 3: Approved Identifiers and Band Placement

### Clause 3.1: Approved Patient Identifiers
Staff must use two explicit patient identifiers to confirm identity:
1. Patient Full Legal Name.
2. Hospital Medical Record Number (MRN) or Date of Birth (DOB).  
*Note: Room number, bed assignment, or physical location are strictly prohibited as patient identifiers.*

### Clause 3.2: Band Application Standards
1. Identification bands must be attached directly to the patient's wrist or ankle immediately upon registration.
2. Specialized alert bands (e.g., Red for Allergy, Yellow for Fall Risk, Purple for DNR) must be placed adjacent to the primary ID band.
3. For pediatric or neonatal patients, two identification bands (wrist and ankle) must be worn at all times.

---

## Section 4: Mandatory Identification Verification Workflow

### Clause 4.1: Active Verification Sequence (Verbal Patients)
Prior to administering medications, drawing blood, taking X-rays, or performing any procedure, staff must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Ask the patient to state their full name and date of birth aloud (e.g., "Please state your full name and date of birth for me").
2. Visually cross-check the verbal response against the patient's physical identification wristband.
3. Compare the details on the wristband directly against the Electronic Health Record (eMAR/order sheet) or barcoded medication label.
4. Scan the barcoded identification band using an approved EHR barcode scanner where available.

### Clause 4.2: Passive Verification Sequence (Non-Verbal / Pediatric / Sedated)
If a patient is pediatric, unconscious, confused, or intubated:
1. Verify details on the identification wristband against the EHR order sheet.
2. Confirm identity verbally with a verified family member or legal proxy present at the bedside.
3. Require two qualified clinicians to co-verify the identification band details against the EHR chart if no family member is present.

---

## Section 5: Missing or Damaged Band Replacement

### Clause 5.1: Missing Band Protocol
If a patient's identification band is missing, damaged, or unreadable, staff must not proceed with any medication or procedure until a replacement band is generated.

### Clause 5.2: Re-Identification Workflow
The primary care nurse must verify patient identity with admission records or a family member, request a replacement band from Registration, and apply the new band immediately.
