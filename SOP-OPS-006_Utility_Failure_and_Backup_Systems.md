# HOSPITAL OPERATIONAL POLICY

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-006  
**Title:** Utility Failure and Back-Up System Activation Protocols  
**Domain:** Hospital Operations  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope

### Clause 1.1: Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines immediate response protocols, auxiliary system switchover sequences, and operational continuity workflows during catastrophic interruptions of critical hospital utilities, including electrical power, municipal water, medical gases, and HVAC systems.

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
This policy applies to facility engineers, emergency command personnel, clinical nursing supervisors, intensive care units, operating room staff, and administrative leads across all hospital facilities.

---

## Section 2: Roles and Responsibilities

### Clause 2.1: Primary Responsible Role
The **Director of Facility Operations / Chief Engineer** is responsible for monitoring primary utility feeds, verifying automatic backup system activations, and managing physical infrastructure repairs.

### Clause 2.2: Clinical Continuity Lead
The **Inpatient Nursing Supervisor / Administrative Director on Call** is responsible for managing clinical load balancing, coordinating portable life-support support, and executing internal evacuation plans if necessary.

---

## Section 3: Backup Infrastructure Specifications

### Clause 3.1: Emergency Power Supply System (EPSS)
1. Critical life-safety circuits and red emergency power receptacles are backed up by emergency diesel generators.
2. Generator automatic transfer switches (ATS) must restore emergency electrical power to red outlets within **10 seconds** of primary utility loss.

### Clause 3.2: Medical Gas Reserve Controls
1. Secondary bulk oxygen reserve manifolds automatically initiate flow if primary liquid oxygen line pressure drops below **40 psi**.
2. Auxiliary zone valve boxes must remain accessible to designated charge nurses at all times.

---

## Section 4: Utility Failure Response Workflows

### Clause 4.1: Electrical Power Outage Response Sequence
Upon sudden loss of main electrical power, staff must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Verify emergency generator activation within 10 seconds (confirm illumination of red emergency outlets).
2. Plug all critical life-sustaining devices (ventilators, infusion pumps, intra-aortic balloon pumps) directly into red emergency power outlets.
3. Obtain portable emergency LED lighting units stored in clean utility rooms for unlit hallways and nursing stations.
4. Assess ventilated patients to confirm continuous mechanical function; switch to manual bag-valve-mask (Ambu bag) ventilation if ventilator battery power drops below critical levels.
5. Facilities staff perform manual inspection of all emergency generator transfer switches and fuel tank levels.
6. Central Switchboard announces "Code Yellow - Utility Emergency (Electrical)" to initiate incident command protocols if outage exceeds 15 minutes.

### Clause 4.2: Medical Oxygen Pipeline Failure Sequence
In the event of sudden main oxygen line pressure loss:
1. Charge Nurse immediately retrieves emergency E-cylinder portable oxygen tanks from the unit storage room.
2. Switch intubated or high-flow oxygen patients from wall outlets to portable E-cylinders.
3. Facility operations manually switch bulk oxygen supply to secondary reserve manifolds.
4. If pipeline rupture occurs within a specific unit, the designated unit nurse manually closes the local zone valve box to isolate the leak.

---

## Section 5: Long-Term Disruption and Reporting

### Clause 5.1: Water Supply Distruption Thresholds
If municipal water failure exceeds **2 hours**, Facilities must activate emergency potable water delivery contracts and deploy portable sanitation units to high-priority inpatient floors.

### Clause 5.2: Post-Restoration System Safety Verification
Following utility restoration, Facility Operations must inspect and certify all mechanical, electrical, and gas distribution lines before clinical leadership authorizes return to standard operations.
