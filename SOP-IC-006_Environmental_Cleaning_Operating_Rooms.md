# HOSPITAL OPERATIONAL POLICY

**Document ID:** SOP-IC-006  
**Title:** Environmental Cleaning and Disinfection of Operating Rooms  
**Domain:** Infection Control  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope

### Clause 1.1: Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines standardized cleaning and disinfection practices for Operating Rooms (OR) to maintain environmental cleanliness, control bioburden, and prevent surgical site infections (SSI).

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
This policy applies to all Environmental Services (EVS) personnel, perioperative support staff, and surgical nurses involved in environmental cleaning before, during, and after surgical procedures.

---

## Section 2: Roles and Responsibilities

### Clause 2.1: Primary Responsible Role
The **EVS Operating Room Team Lead** is responsible for executing environmental cleaning protocols, verifying surface disinfection contact times, and documenting daily cleanliness logs.

### Clause 2.2: Perioperative Oversight
The **Operating Room Charge Nurse** verifies that turnover cleaning is complete and inspected prior to permitting the next surgical patient to enter the room.

---

## Section 3: Cleaning Categories and Schedules

### Clause 3.1: First-of-the-Day Damp Dusting
Before the first scheduled surgical procedure of the day, EVS staff must damp-dust all horizontal surfaces (surgical lights, boom arms, surgical tables, equipment carts) using a clean microfiber cloth moistened with EPA-registered hospital disinfectant.

### Clause 3.2: Intraoperative Spot Cleaning
During a surgical procedure, any accidental blood or body fluid spills must be promptly contained and spot-cleaned by designated staff using an approved disinfectant.

---

## Section 4: Between-Case (Turnover) Cleaning Procedure

### Clause 4.1: Turnover Execution Sequence
EVS personnel must perform the following sequential steps during room turnover between surgical procedures:
1. Don appropriate protective apparel, including fluid-resistant gloves and eye protection.
2. Collect and remove all regulated medical waste (biohazard bags) and linen bags, sealing them prior to transport.
3. Clean and disinfect all high-touch surfaces, including surgical lights, anesthesia carts, operating table pads, positioning devices, electrosurgical units, and suction canisters.
4. Clean the floor surrounding the operating table within a 3-to-4-foot perimeter, or further if visible contamination exists.
5. Apply EPA-registered intermediate-level disinfectant to all cleaned surfaces, ensuring the surface remains visibly wet for the full manufacturer-recommended **wet contact time (minimum 3 to 5 minutes)**.
6. Remove gloves, perform hand hygiene, and allow surfaces to completely air dry before placing fresh linens on the operating table.

---

## Section 5: Terminal (End-of-Day) Cleaning and Quality Audits

### Clause 5.1: Terminal Cleaning Scope
At the end of each working day, every operating room must undergo terminal cleaning, regardless of whether it was used. This includes scrubbing all walls, ceilings, light fixtures, scrub sinks, overhead tracks, and total floor flooding/mopping with a germicidal disinfectant solution.

### Clause 5.2: Fluorescent Marker Audits
The EVS Team Lead must perform weekly random visual and fluorescent marker audits on high-touch surfaces in at least 25% of operating rooms to evaluate cleaning thoroughness and provide feedback to EVS staff.
