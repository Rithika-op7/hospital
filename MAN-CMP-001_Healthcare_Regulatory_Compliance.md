# HOSPITAL OPERATIONAL POLICY

**Document ID:** MAN-CMP-001  
**Title:** Healthcare Regulatory Compliance Program and Fraud Prevention  
**Domain:** Compliance Manual  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope

### Clause 1.1: Objective
This Manual (MAN) establishes structural requirements, operational standards, and oversight mechanisms for the hospital's corporate compliance program to ensure adherence to healthcare laws, prevent fraudulent billing, and promote ethical organizational conduct.

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
This manual applies to all executive leadership, clinical staff, administrative employees, billing contractors, medical staff members, and vendors across all entities and subsidiaries operating under the hospital management system.

---

## Section 2: Roles and Responsibilities

### Clause 2.1: Primary Responsible Role
The **Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)** is responsible for overseeing the corporate compliance program, conducting internal audits, investigating non-compliance reports, and reporting directly to the Board of Directors.

### Clause 2.2: Management Implementation Lead
**Department Heads and Clinical Managers** are responsible for ensuring operational compliance within their respective units, enforcing standard operating procedures, and completing mandatory annual compliance reviews.

---

## Section 3: Core Compliance Elements and Statutory Frameworks

### Clause 3.1: Fundamental Compliance Elements
The hospital compliance structure incorporates the seven core elements of an effective compliance program:
1. Written policies, procedures, and standards of conduct.
2. Designation of a Chief Compliance Officer and Compliance Committee.
3. Effective education and training programs for all personnel.
4. Open communication channels, including an anonymous reporting hotline.
5. Continuous internal auditing and risk monitoring.
6. Enforced disciplinary standards for compliance violations.
7. Prompt response and corrective action for detected offenses.

### Clause 3.2: Fraud, Waste, and Abuse (FWA) Regulations
Staff must strictly comply with federal statutory frameworks:
1. **False Claims Act (FCA):** Prohibits submitting false or fraudulent claims for reimbursement.
2. **Anti-Kickback Statute (AKS):** Prohibits offering, paying, soliciting, or receiving remuneration to induce referrals for items/services payable by healthcare programs.
3. **Stark Law (Physician Self-Referral):** Prohibits physicians from referring patients for designated health services to entities with which they have a financial relationship.

## Section 4: Incident Investigation and Corrective Action Workflow

### Clause 4.1: Compliance Non-Compliance Handling Sequence
When a potential compliance violation or billing irregularity is reported, the Compliance Office must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Log the complaint into the Compliance Audit Database within **24 hours** of receipt.
2. Initiate a preliminary factual review to determine the scope and validity of the allegation.
3. Notify the Office of Legal Counsel if potential statutory violations (e.g., FCA, AKS) are implicated.
4. Conduct interviews with involved personnel and review financial, clinical, or billing records.
5. Formulate a formal **Corrective Action Plan (CAP)** addressing root causes within **30 calendar days**.
6. Execute repayments to payers (government or commercial) for any identified overpayments within statutory timelines (**60 days** of identification).
7. Issue appropriate disciplinary sanctions ranging from mandatory re-education to employment termination.

---

## Section 5: Reporting Hotline and Whistleblower Protections

### Clause 5.1: Non-Retaliation Policy
The hospital maintains a strict **Zero Tolerance Non-Retaliation Policy**. Retaliation against any employee who reports a good-faith concern regarding illegal, unethical, or non-compliant conduct is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate termination.

### Clause 5.2: Anonymous Reporting Channels
Personnel may report compliance concerns 24/7 via the toll-free Compliance Hotline or the web-based anonymous portal managed by an independent third-party provider.
