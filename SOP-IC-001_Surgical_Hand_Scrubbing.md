# HOSPITAL OPERATIONAL POLICY & SOP MANUAL

**Document ID:** SOP-IC-001  
**Title:** Surgical Hand Scrubbing and Antisepsis Protocol  
**Domain:** Infection Control  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope

### Clause 1.1: Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the mandatory protocol for surgical hand antisepsis to minimize microbial counts on the hands and forearms of surgical team members prior to performing or assisting in invasive surgical procedures.

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
This protocol applies strictly to all operating room personnel, including attending surgeons, surgical residents, scrub nurses, physician assistants, and surgical technologists entering the sterile surgical field.

---

## Section 2: Roles and Responsibilities

### Clause 2.1: Primary Responsible Role
The **Scrub Nurse** is responsible for monitoring compliance with the surgical scrub procedure at the scrub sink and ensuring that all personnel maintain sterile technique prior to gowning and gloving.

### Clause 2.2: Individual Responsibility
Every surgical team member is individually accountable for executing the full scrub duration and maintaining hand hygiene integrity.

---

## Section 3: Pre-Scrub Requirements

### Clause 3.1: Preparation Steps
Before commencing the surgical hand scrub, personnel must complete the following preparation sequence:
1. Remove all jewelry, rings, watches, and wrist accessories.
2. Inspect hands and forearms for cuts, abrasions, or non-intact skin; personnel with open skin lesions must be restricted from surgical procedures.
3. Ensure fingernails are clean, natural, short (less than 1/4 inch), and free of artificial nails or cracked polish.
4. Don appropriate Operating Room Attire, including surgical cap, mask covering nose and mouth, and protective eye equipment.

---

## Section 4: Surgical Hand Scrub Procedure

### Clause 4.1: Water and Scrub Agent Selection
Personnel may use either an approved antimicrobial scrub agent (4% Chlorhexidine Gluconate or 7.5% Povidone-Iodine) with water or an approved alcohol-based surgical hand rub (ABSHR) if hands are free of visible soil.

### Clause 4.2: Timed Scrub Method (Water-Based)
When using water and an antimicrobial agent, personnel must execute the following sequential steps:
1. Turn on water using foot or sensor controls and adjust to a warm temperature.
2. Pre-wash hands and forearms up to 2 inches above the elbows with plain soap and water.
3. Clean subungual areas (under fingernails) under running water using a single-use disposable nail cleaner.
4. Apply 5 mL of antimicrobial scrub solution to hands and forearms.
5. Scrub hands and forearms for a total continuous duration of **5 minutes** for the first scrub of the day, and **3 minutes** for subsequent procedures.
6. Scrub systematically using anatomical zones: scrub all 4 surfaces of each finger, hand, wrist, and forearm up to 2 inches above the elbow.
7. Rinse hands and forearms thoroughly under running water in one direction, moving from fingertips to elbows, keeping hands elevated above elbows at all times.
8. Dry hands thoroughly using a sterile towel, using separate dedicated ends of the towel for each arm.

### Clause 4.3: Alcohol-Based Surgical Hand Rub (ABSHR) Method
When using an alcohol-based rub, personnel must:
1. Wash hands with plain soap and water if visibly soiled, then dry completely.
2. Apply the manufacturer-recommended volume (typically 2 pump actuations) of ABSHR to clean, dry hands.
3. Rub hands, wrists, and forearms up to the elbows until completely dry, maintaining wet contact for a minimum duration of **3 minutes**.

---

## Section 5: Post-Scrub Compliance & Actions

### Clause 5.1: Sterile Integrity Maintenance
After completion of the scrub sequence, hands must be kept above waist level and away from surgical attire. Personnel must proceed directly to the operating room for gowning and gloving without touching non-sterile objects (doors, sink edges, lights).

### Clause 5.2: Re-Scrub Requirement
If any unsterile object or non-sterile surface is touched at any point prior to gowning, the surgical team member must immediately perform a full re-scrub according to Clause 4.2.
