DOCUMENT: MAN-CMP-001
DOMAIN: Compliance Manual
SECTION: 4 - Incident Investigation and Corrective Action Workflow
CLAUSE: 4.1 - Compliance Non-Compliance Handling Sequence

CONTENT:
When a potential compliance violation or billing irregularity is reported, the Compliance Office must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Log the complaint into the Compliance Audit Database within **24 hours** of receipt.
2. Initiate a preliminary factual review to determine the scope and validity of the allegation.
3. Notify the Office of Legal Counsel if potential statutory violations (e.g., FCA, AKS) are implicated.
4. Conduct interviews with involved personnel and review financial, clinical, or billing records.
5. Formulate a formal **Corrective Action Plan (CAP)** addressing root causes within **30 calendar days**.
6. Execute repayments to payers (government or commercial) for any identified overpayments within statutory timelines (**60 days** of identification).
7. Issue appropriate disciplinary sanctions ranging from mandatory re-education to employment termination.

INDEX: 16
DOCUMENT: MAN-CMP-002
DOMAIN: Compliance Manual
SECTION: 4 - Data Safeguards and Breach Incident Response Workflow
CLAUSE: 4.2 - Breach Identification and Escalation Workflow

CONTENT:
When an unauthorized access, disclosure, or theft of PHI is suspected, staff must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Disconnect compromised devices from the local network and notify the IT Helpdesk and Privacy Office immediately.
2. The Privacy Officer logs the incident and initiates a four-factor risk assessment to evaluate:
   - Nature and extent of PHI involved.
   - Unauthorized person who used or received the info.
   - Whether PHI was actually viewed or acquired.
   - Extent to which risk has been mitigated.
3. If the risk assessment confirms a reportable breach, notify affected individuals in writing without unreasonable delay and no later than **60 calendar days** following discovery.
4. If a breach affects **500 or more individuals**, notify the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) and prominent media outlets concurrently.

7. Complete an online Safety Incident Report and Workplace Violence Event Form within **12 hours** of the incident.

SECTION: 4 - Informed Consent Execution Workflow
CLAUSE: 4.1 - Standard Consent Workflow

CONTENT:
Prior to transferring a patient to the procedure area, staff must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. The attending physician conducts the consent discussion with the patient or legal surrogate.
2. Provide the patient or surrogate the opportunity to ask clarifying questions.
3. Obtain the physical or electronic signature of the patient or legally authorized representative (LAR).
4. The witness signs and dates the consent form, confirming the signature was executed voluntarily.
5. Upload or place the completed consent form into the active patient electronic medical record (EMR).
6. The perioperative nurse verifies that the consent form matches the scheduled procedure during pre-op intake.

INDEX: 51
DOCUMENT: POL-CNS-002
DOMAIN: Consent Procedures
SECTION: 3 - Statutory Emergency Exception Criteria
CLAUSE: 3.1 - Criteria for Presumed Consent

CONTENT:
Emergency treatment without explicit written informed consent is authorized only when all of the following criteria are met simultaneously:
1. The patient suffers from an immediate life-threatening condition or threat of severe, irreversible organ dysfunction.
2. The patient lacks decision-making capacity due to unconsciousness, severe trauma, altered mental status, or acute delirium.
3. No legally authorized representative (surrogate, power of attorney, or immediate family) is immediately available to provide consent.
4. A reasonable person under similar circumstances would consent to the life-saving intervention.

INDEX: 53
DOCUMENT: POL-CNS-002

SECTION: 4 - Emergency Non-Consent Execution Workflow
CLAUSE: 4.1 - Emergency Intervention Sequence

CONTENT:
When encountering an incapacitated patient requiring urgent intervention, clinicians must execute the following sequential steps:
1. Attempt immediate verbal or telephonic contact with next of kin or designated legal surrogate.
2. If the surrogate cannot be reached without delaying life-saving treatment, initiate the "Emergency Exception Protocol."
3. Obtain verbal confirmation and second signature from a second independent attending physician.
4. Proceed immediately with necessary surgical, diagnostic, or therapeutic interventions.
5. Enter a detailed clinical note into the EHR titled "Emergency Consent Exception Form" within **2 hours** post-procedure, detailing the life threat and surrogate search efforts.
6. Continue attempts to contact family members and disclose performed emergency procedures as soon as possible.

DOMAIN: Consent Procedures
SECTION: 4 - Treatment Refusal and AMA Execution Workflow
CLAUSE: 4.1 - AMA Discharge Execution Sequence

CONTENT:
When a competent patient requests to discontinue care or leave the facility against medical advice, staff must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Notify the attending physician immediately upon patient request to leave.
2. The attending physician conducts an in-person bedside consultation to discuss underlying patient concerns (e.g., pain, anxiety, financial/family issues) and explore compromise management options.
3. If the patient maintains their decision to leave, the physician must explicitly warn the patient of specific medical risks, potential complications, permanent injury, or mortality.
4. Present the formal **"Discharge Against Medical Advice (AMA) Form"** for patient signature.
5. If the patient signed or verbally refused to sign, the nurse and physician must co-sign as witnesses.
6. Provide the patient with standard discharge instructions, appropriate outpatient prescriptions (unless clinically unsafe), and follow-up clinic contact information.
7. Document the full capacity assessment, risk discussion details, and witness names in the EHR.

INDEX: 266
DOCUMENT: SOP-OPS-006
DOMAIN: Hospital Operations
SECTION: 4 - Utility Failure Response Workflows
CLAUSE: 4.1 - Electrical Power Outage Response Sequence

CONTENT:
Upon sudden loss of main electrical power, staff must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Verify emergency generator activation within 10 seconds (confirm illumination of red emergency outlets).
2. Plug all critical life-sustaining devices (ventilators, infusion pumps, intra-aortic balloon pumps) directly into red emergency power outlets.
3. Obtain portable emergency LED lighting units stored in clean utility rooms for unlit hallways and nursing stations.
4. Assess ventilated patients to confirm continuous mechanical function; switch to manual bag-valve-mask (Ambu bag) ventilation if ventilator battery power drops below critical levels.
5. Facilities staff perform manual inspection of all emergency generator transfer switches and fuel tank levels.
6. Central Switchboard announces "Code Yellow - Utility Emergency (Electrical)" to initiate incident command protocols if outage exceeds 15 minutes.

================================================================================
INDEX: 267
DOCUMENT: SOP-OPS-006
DOMAIN: Hospital Operations
SECTION: 4 - Utility Failure Response Workflows
CLAUSE: 4.2 - Medical Oxygen Pipeline Failure Sequence

CONTENT:
In the event of sudden main oxygen line pressure loss:
1. Charge Nurse immediately retrieves emergency E-cylinder portable oxygen tanks from the unit storage room.
2. Switch intubated or high-flow oxygen patients from wall outlets to portable E-cylinders.
3. Facility operations manually switch bulk oxygen supply to secondary reserve manifolds.
4. If pipeline rupture occurs within a specific unit, the designated unit nurse manually closes the local zone valve box to isolate the leak.

INDEX: 276
DOCUMENT: SOP-OPS-007
DOMAIN: Hospital Operations
SECTION: 4 - Visitor Intake and Restricted Area Access Workflow
CLAUSE: 4.1 - Visitor Verification and Badge Issuance Workflow

CONTENT:
Upon arrival at any hospital entrance after general hours or at specialized unit portals, staff must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Security or reception staff request government-issued photo identification from the visitor.
2. Search the EHR visitor management module to confirm the patient permits visitors and has not placed a "Do Not Disclose" security restriction on their file.
3. Log visitor full name, contact number, and destination room into the electronic Visitor Management System.
4. Capture visitor photo and print an adhesive Visitor Pass Badge displaying the current date, visitor name, and designated destination.
5. Instruct the visitor to apply the badge prominently to their outerwear.
6. Verify badge validity at unit access points via electronic scanner or visual inspection prior to granting entry.
