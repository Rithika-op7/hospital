# HOSPITAL OPERATIONAL POLICY

**Document ID:** SOP-IC-002  
**Title:** Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Selection in Isolation Wards  
**Domain:** Infection Control  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope

### Clause 1.1: Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines mandatory protocols for selecting, donning, doffing, and disposing of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) when caring for patients under isolation precautions to prevent healthcare-associated transmission of pathogens.

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
This policy applies to all clinical personnel, including registered nurses, attending physicians, physical therapists, and environmental services staff entering contact, droplet, or airborne isolation rooms.

---

## Section 2: Roles and Responsibilities

### Clause 2.1: Primary Responsible Role
The **Isolation Ward Unit Nurse** is responsible for verifying that all individuals entering the isolation zone select and correctly don the appropriate level of PPE prior to entry.

### Clause 2.2: Compliance Supervision
The **Hospital Infection Control Officer** conducts unannounced weekly audits to ensure full adherence to PPE doffing and disposal sequences.

---

## Section 3: Isolation Tiers and Required Equipment

### Clause 3.1: Contact Isolation Precautions
Personnel entering rooms designated for Contact Precautions (e.g., MRSA, C. difficile) must wear clean, non-sterile gloves and a fluid-resistant isolation gown.

### Clause 3.2: Droplet Isolation Precautions
Personnel entering rooms designated for Droplet Precautions (e.g., Influenza, Pertussis) must wear a surgical mask with eye protection (goggles or full-face shield) upon entry.

### Clause 3.3: Airborne Isolation Precautions
Personnel entering Airborne Infection Isolation Rooms (AIIR) (e.g., Tuberculosis, Measles) must wear a fit-tested N95 filtering facepiece respirator or Powered Air-Purifying Respirator (PAPR), fluid-resistant gown, and gloves.

---

## Section 4: Donning and Doffing Procedure

### Clause 4.1: Donning Sequence (Prior to Entry)
All staff must execute the following sequential steps in the designated clean anteroom before entering an isolation room:
1. Perform hand hygiene using an alcohol-based hand rub or soap and water.
2. Put on the isolation gown, ensuring full torso coverage, and fasten securely at the neck and waist.
3. Put on the appropriate mask or N95 respirator, securing ties/straps and forming the nosepiece over the bridge of the nose.
4. Put on protective eyewear or face shield.
5. Put on disposable gloves, extending the glove cuffs over the wrists of the gown.

### Clause 4.2: Doffing Sequence (Prior to Exit)
All staff must execute the following sequential doffing steps at the doorway or in the anteroom to avoid self-contamination:
1. Remove gloves using the glove-in-glove technique and dispose of them immediately in the biohazard receptacle.
2. Unfasten gown ties and gently peel the gown away from neck and shoulders, touching only the inside of the gown, roll it inside-out, and dispose of it.
3. Perform hand hygiene using an alcohol-based hand rub.
4. Remove face shield or goggles by touching the headband/ear pieces only (do not touch the front).
5. Remove mask or respirator by touching the bottom ties/straps first, then top straps, and discard without touching the front.
6. Perform final hand hygiene using soap and water for at least 20 seconds.

---

## Section 5: PPE Waste Disposal and Escalation

### Clause 5.1: Biohazardous Waste Handling
All single-use PPE used inside an isolation room must be discarded directly into yellow biohazard waste bags located within the room or anteroom.

### Clause 5.2: PPE Breach Protocol
If a glove tear, gown saturation, or respirator seal failure occurs inside the isolation ward, the staff member must immediately exit the patient room, perform hand hygiene, replace the compromised equipment, and report the exposure to the Unit Nurse.
