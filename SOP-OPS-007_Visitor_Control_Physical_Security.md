# HOSPITAL OPERATIONAL POLICY

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-007  
**Title:** Visitor Control and Physical Security Protocols  
**Domain:** Hospital Operations  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope

### Clause 1.1: Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines physical security standards, visitor access control procedures, badge identification requirements, and restricted area protocols to ensure a safe environment for patients, staff, and visitors.

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
This policy applies to all security officers, reception staff, clinical nursing leads, facility management, and administrative personnel across all hospital access portals, inpatient wards, and specialized care units.

---

## Section 2: Roles and Responsibilities

### Clause 2.1: Primary Responsible Role
The **Director of Security / Security Officers** are responsible for managing perimeter access checkpoints, issuing visitor passes, monitoring electronic surveillance networks, and responding to physical security threats.

### Clause 2.2: Unit Access Controller
The **Unit Clerk / Charge Nurse** is responsible for verifying visitor credentials at specialized care unit entrances, enforcing unit visiting hours, and monitoring visitor compliance within patient care areas.

---

## Section 3: Visitor Access Regulations and Hours

### Clause 3.1: Visiting Hours and Limits
1. General inpatient visiting hours are established from **08:00 to 20:00 daily**.
2. Standard inpatient units permit a maximum of **two (2) visitors** per patient at any single time.
3. Intensive Care Units (ICU) and Emergency Departments permit a maximum of **one (1) visitor** at the bedside, subject to clinical team discretion.

### Clause 3.2: Visitor Identification Requirements
All visitors entering hospital facilities must present a valid government-issued photo ID at main security reception checkpoints to receive a photo-printed **Visitor Pass Badge**. The badge must be worn visibly above the waist at all times while on hospital premises.

---

## Section 4: Visitor Intake and Restricted Area Access Workflow

### Clause 4.1: Visitor Verification and Badge Issuance Workflow
Upon arrival at any hospital entrance after general hours or at specialized unit portals, staff must execute the following sequential workflow:
1. Security or reception staff request government-issued photo identification from the visitor.
2. Search the EHR visitor management module to confirm the patient permits visitors and has not placed a "Do Not Disclose" security restriction on their file.
3. Log visitor full name, contact number, and destination room into the electronic Visitor Management System.
4. Capture visitor photo and print an adhesive Visitor Pass Badge displaying the current date, visitor name, and designated destination.
5. Instruct the visitor to apply the badge prominently to their outerwear.
6. Verify badge validity at unit access points via electronic scanner or visual inspection prior to granting entry.

### Clause 4.2: Restricted Area Access Controls
1. Specialized clinical areas—including Newborn Nurseries, Labor & Delivery, Operating Suites, and Pharmacy Vaults—remain under continuous 24/7 electronic card-access lock.
2. Visitors accessing high-risk units (e.g., Neonatal ICU) must be issued specialized proximity access cards following verification against the authorized parental/guardian list.

---

## Section 5: Security Violations and Visitor Removal

### Clause 5.1: Revocation of Visiting Privileges
Security officers or clinical supervisors may immediately revoke visiting privileges and escort visitors off hospital premises for belligerent behavior, intoxication, failure to adhere to infection control PPE rules, or refusal to display visitor badges.

### Clause 5.2: After-Hours Lockout Protocol
At **20:00 daily**, main public access doors lock automatically. Security officers perform sweep inspections of public concourses, and all subsequent entries must process through the Emergency Department main security screening station.
